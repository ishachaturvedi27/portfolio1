### Control Structures 
Control structures helped organize how our game woked by controlling how each action was being done during gameplay. They allowed the game to react to palyer movement, enemy action, and more. 

Here are some examples of where we used them:

- checking if te player collided with anything
- creating loops for repeating actions to avoid writing it again and again. 
- using conditions to control level progression
- managing player movement and keyboard controls 

### Iterations 
Iterations were a key tactic of our game becuase they allowed certain actions to repeat automatically saving our time and effort. This allowed for a better game expereince and smooth gameplay.

Here are some examples of where we used them:

- repeating enemy movements 
- repeating animations for players/enemies/npcs
- lopping through game objects like coins and obstacles 
- creating repeated actions such as background movement or scoring systems 

### Conditionals 
Conditionals were used throughout our game to make decisions based on different situations during gameplay. They helped the game respond to player actions, events, and changing conditions in each level.

Here are some places where conditionals were used:

- checking if the player won or lost the game
- detecting collisions with enemies, platforms, or items
- controlling when levels start, end, or change
- responding to keyboard inputs and player actions
